FAQ

A few useful boundaries.

Do I need to know what I want automated?

No. A good starting point is simply a recurring computer-based task that consumes more employee time than it should. The initial consultation is meant to determine whether there is a realistic opportunity.

Is this employee replacement consulting?

The focus is reducing repetitive computer work and recovering employee capacity. Human judgment, approvals, relationship management, and exception handling stay with people wherever they are needed.

Which AI tools do you work with?

Current implementations are centered on ChatGPT and compatible business tools. Fit depends on your existing systems, access, security requirements, and the workflow itself.

Do you guarantee a specific savings amount?

No. Any savings or ROI examples are illustrative until an actual workflow is observed and measured. Feasibility and results vary by process, software, adoption, and business conditions.

Do you train large groups?

That is not the current service model. MindWise engagements are designed around hands-on implementation with an owner, department lead, individual employee, or very small team.
