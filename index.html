Moving it is easy. Getting it onto the truck is the hard part.

A dolly gets the load to the tailgate. Then somebody has to lift it. That’s where backs get hurt, gear gets dropped, and jobs wait on a second person.

  • One person, start to finish

    Pick up, move, raise, and unload without calling in help.

  • Floor to tailgate

    The deck lifts the load toward truck, van, and dock height instead of your back doing it.

  • Slide it, don’t shove it

    Rollers in the deck carry the load across onto the bed.
