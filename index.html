How it works

A dolly gets the load to the truck. Buddy also gets it onto the truck, without a second person.

  1. 1

    Roll it

    Tip the load back and move it like a hand truck.

  2. 2

    Lay it flat

    Lower the frame into a cart to line up at the tailgate or dock.

  3. 3

    Raise and slide

    Lift the deck to bed height and roll the load across on its rollers.
