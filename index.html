Moving it is easy. Loading it is the hard part.

A dolly gets a heavy item to the truck. Then someone has to lift it, tip it, and shove it the last few feet. That’s where backs get hurt, items get dropped, and jobs wait on a second person.

The lift

Getting from the floor to bed height still comes down to muscle.

The transfer

Angling and sliding a load onto a raised surface is slow and awkward.

The helper

Most tools handle transport, not the whole load-and-unload sequence — so you wait for help.

Meet Buddy

Buddy combines a hand truck, a flat cart, and a lift with rollers in one frame. It’s built around one idea: a single person should be able to handle the whole job, from pickup to drop-off.

  • One person, start to finish

  • Floor to tailgate

How it works

Buddy changes position for each stage of the job.

  1. Step 1

    Roll it

    Dolly mode. Tip the item back and move it like a familiar hand truck.

  2. Step 2

    Lay it down

    Cart mode. Bring the frame flatter to position the load at ground level.

  3. Step 3

    Raise and transfer

    Load mode. Lift to the surface height and roll the item across.
