Why carry Buddy

Most of your customers already own a dolly. Very few own something that also gets the load up onto the truck. Buddy is built for that gap — the lift and transfer at the end of the move, done by one person.

It’s a simple story to sell to anyone who loads alone, and it gives you something new to put in front of customers you already serve.
